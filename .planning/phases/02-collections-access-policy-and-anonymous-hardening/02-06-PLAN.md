## Phase Goal

**As a** deployer running a website for a small organisation, **I want to** define collections in config, let the website submit forms to hardened anonymous create endpoints, and manage the records through API-key-protected CRUD with default-deny access, **so that** form submissions land safely in the system of record without me running a CMS.

Source: ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal and success criteria 1-5, restated in user-story form. This plan delivers the honeypot clause of success criterion 2 and the oversized-body and content-type clauses of success criterion 5.

<objective>
User story slice: a website form can post to the anonymous endpoint while bots that fill the hidden field, oversized bodies, non-JSON posts and schemas with unbounded strings are all stopped.

Purpose: SEC-03 and SEC-06, implementing D-08 and the SEC-03 discretion item.
Output: bodyLimit and honeypot config keys, startup checks, media-type hook, honeypot handling, tests.
</objective>
