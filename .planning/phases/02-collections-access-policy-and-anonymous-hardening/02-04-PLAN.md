## Phase Goal

**As a** deployer running a website for a small organisation, **I want to** define collections in config, let the website submit forms to hardened anonymous create endpoints, and manage the records through API-key-protected CRUD with default-deny access, **so that** form submissions land safely in the system of record without me running a CMS.

Source: ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal and success criteria 1-5, restated in user-story form. This plan delivers the rest of success criterion 3: replace a record (PUT), merge-patch it (RFC 7396, validated after merge) and delete it.

<objective>
User story slice: a backend with a key can correct a submitted record, update part of it safely and delete it for good.

Purpose: COLL-05 and COLL-06, plus the ETag part of COLL-04, implementing D-13, D-14 and D-20.
Output: merge-patch and If-Match helpers, RecordStore.replace and remove, PUT/PATCH/DELETE routes, tests.
</objective>

Records are `{ _id, _v, createdAt, updatedAt, data }` (plan 02-01). PUT and PATCH replace `data` only; `_id` and `createdAt` never change. The research Pattern 8 sketch spreads the body into `$set` at the top level, which would leave old fields behind on PUT and let a body overwrite server fields; this plan sets `data` as a whole instead.

ETag is `"<_v>"`. The version is the only concurrency token; no payload hashing (research Pitfall 8).
